Task Drop — config QR

Builds the one-time QR that hands your endpoint to the R1. Scan it instead of typing a long URL on the device keyboard.

This QR contains your key. Everything here runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded and nothing is saved. Don’t screenshot it into a shared album, don’t commit it, and close this tab when you’re done.

Scan with your R1. Task Drop installs, reads the config, saves it to secure storage, then strips it from the URL.

How this works / if the scan doesn’t carry the config

The QR holds the R1 install-card JSON. Its url is your creation plus a fragment: #cfg=<base64 of {"u":"<exec url>","k":"<key>"}>. On first load Task Drop reads that fragment, writes it to creationStorage.secure, and calls history.replaceState so the key is no longer in the address.

If your R1 firmware drops the fragment when it installs the card, nothing is lost — open Task Drop, touch and hold the screen for a second, and type the URL and key into the setup screen directly.
